Who I Am

I'm an independent creative developer with a passion for building handcrafted digital experiences that blend motion, interactivity, and thoughtful design.

With over a decade of experience in front-end engineering and creative technology, I specialize in creating immersive web experiences that push the boundaries of what's possible in the browser. I'm addicted to well thought-through animations and smart user-experiences.

When I'm not building for the web, I explore generative art — creating algorithms that generate visuals for screens and the physical world.
